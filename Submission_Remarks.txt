Employee Performance Mapping - Course-End Project 1
Prepared by Heba Farouk, Business Analyst, Sparrow Softech Pvt. Ltd.

This project demonstrates practical SQL application for HR analytics using the ScienceQtech case study. The solution covers database preparation, employee and department reporting, performance-rating segmentation, leadership identification, departmental rating benchmarking, role-wise salary analysis, employee experience ranking, SQL views, indexing and execution-plan analysis, geographic salary distribution, subqueries, training-review support and role standardization.

Note: Actual output screenshots must be generated after importing the official Reference Materials CSV datasets. No employee/project records were fabricated in this package.
